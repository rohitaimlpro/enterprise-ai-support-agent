# Feedback, Reviews & Communication Preferences

Covers giving us feedback (reviews and complaints) and controlling what
we send you (newsletters, notifications). For the mechanics of escalating
an active support issue, see Contact & Escalation.

## Leaving a review

Rate us from **Account Settings > Feedback > Leave a Review**, or reply
to the review-request email sent about 2 weeks after you first
subscribe -- that's timed to land after you've had a chance to actually
use the product, not immediately at signup. Reviews are a star rating
plus optional written comments, and go straight to our product team
(they're not customer-facing on a public page today, unlike a
third-party review site). You can update your review at any time; only
your most recent submission counts.

## Filing a complaint

For a specific bad experience (a bug, a billing mistake, a support
interaction that didn't go well), file a support ticket or ask the
assistant to escalate -- this is routed differently from a general
review so a support lead follows up directly, typically within the
response times listed in Contact & Escalation. The distinction that
matters: a **review** is general product feedback that doesn't need a
reply; a **complaint** is a specific incident that does need a reply and
resolution.

If your complaint is about a billing charge specifically, it's usually
faster to start from the relevant billing flow (see Payments & Invoices
or Refund Policy) so it's categorized correctly from the start, rather
than a general complaint ticket that then needs to be re-routed.

## What we do with feedback

Product feedback from reviews feeds into our roadmap prioritization --
we don't reply to every review individually, but recurring themes
directly influence what gets built next. Complaints, by contrast, always
get an individual response since they represent an unresolved issue for
that specific customer.

## Newsletter and marketing emails

Manage what you receive under **Account Settings > Notifications > Email
Preferences**. Categories you can toggle independently:

- **Product newsletter**: monthly roundup of tips and updates.
- **Feature announcements**: sent when we ship something new, roughly
  every 2-4 weeks.
- **Promotional offers**: discounts, seasonal sales, referral programs.
- **Community/events**: webinars, user-group invites (mainly relevant to
  Team/Enterprise admins).

Transactional emails (receipts, security alerts, password resets, ticket
updates) can't be turned off, since they're required for account
security and billing records -- these aren't marketing and aren't
covered by the preferences above.

## Unsubscribing from the newsletter

Click **Unsubscribe** at the bottom of any newsletter email, or toggle
it off in **Email Preferences** -- both take effect immediately (within
one send cycle) and don't affect your subscription or account status in
any way. Unsubscribing from the newsletter specifically only disables
that one category; if you want to opt out of everything non-essential,
toggle off each category individually or use "Unsubscribe from all
marketing" at the bottom of Email Preferences.

## Frequently asked questions

**Will leaving a negative review affect my account or support
priority?**
No -- reviews are anonymized before reaching the product team and have
no connection to support ticket routing or priority.

**Can Team/Enterprise admins manage notification preferences for the
whole team?**
Admins can set a default for new members from **Admin Console >
Notifications**, but each member can still override it for their own
account under their personal Email Preferences.

**If I unsubscribe from marketing, will I still get told about a price
change?**
Yes -- required account/billing notices (like the 30-day price-change
notice mentioned in Pricing Guide) are transactional, not marketing, and
always send regardless of your newsletter preferences.
