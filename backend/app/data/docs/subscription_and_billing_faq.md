# Subscription & Billing FAQ

General questions about how billing works day to day. For the actual
prices, see Pricing Guide. For getting money back, see Refund Policy. For
managing saved cards and viewing invoices, see Payments & Invoices.

## How do I upgrade my Meridian Suite subscription?

Go to **Account Settings > Plan & Billing > Change Plan**. You can move
from the Individual plan to the Team or Enterprise plan at any time. The
price difference for the current billing period is charged immediately
(prorated); future invoices reflect the new plan's full price. Upgrades
take effect immediately -- you get the new plan's features (more seats,
admin console, SSO, etc.) as soon as the charge succeeds, you don't wait
for the next billing cycle.

Example: you're on Individual ($19.99/month) with 20 days left in the
billing cycle and switch to Team ($27.99/seat/month, 2 seats). You're
charged a prorated amount for the difference for the remaining 20 days on
the new plan, then billed the full $55.98/month (2 seats) going forward.

## How do I downgrade my plan?

Downgrades take effect at the **end of your current billing cycle**, so
you keep access to premium features you already paid for through that
date -- there's no prorated refund for downgrading mid-cycle (see Refund
Policy for what is refundable). You cannot downgrade below the number of
active seats on a Team or Enterprise plan; remove seats first from
**Admin Console > Seats** before downgrading the plan tier itself.

Downgrading from Team/Enterprise to Individual also removes multi-seat
features (admin console, SSO, shared team libraries) at the downgrade
date -- export or reassign anything team-owned before then.

## Can I switch between monthly and annual billing?

Yes. Switching from monthly to annual applies the annual discount
(roughly 16% off, see Pricing Guide for exact numbers) starting on your
**next renewal date**, not immediately -- you finish out the current
monthly cycle first. Switching from annual back to monthly is only
allowed once your current annual term ends, since annual pricing is
contingent on the 12-month commitment.

## What payment methods are supported?

Credit/debit card, PayPal, and (Enterprise plans only) invoiced bank
transfer with NET-30 terms. See Payments & Invoices for the full list of
accepted card networks and what to do if a payment fails.

## Why was I charged twice this month?

This usually happens when a plan change and a scheduled renewal fall in
the same billing cycle -- for example, upgrading mid-cycle generates a
prorated charge, and then your normal renewal charge follows a few days
later on schedule. Check **Account Settings > Billing History** for an
itemized breakdown; each charge lists what it's for (proration, renewal,
add-on, etc.). If the charges still look wrong after reviewing that, file
a support ticket with both charge IDs and we'll investigate within 2
business days.

## How do seats work on Team and Enterprise plans?

Each seat is one named user with their own login -- seats can't be shared
between two people, both for licensing and security reasons (see Account
Security FAQ). Adding a seat mid-cycle charges a prorated amount for the
remainder of the cycle; removing a seat takes effect at the next renewal
and doesn't generate a partial-period refund. Enterprise plans can set a
"seat pool" that admins assign and reassign without contacting support.

## Can I pause my subscription instead of cancelling?

There's no formal pause feature today -- the closest equivalent is
downgrading to the lowest-cost plan (Individual) for the period you don't
need Team/Enterprise features, then upgrading again later. Your files and
history are retained as long as the account itself isn't deleted, even
on a lower plan tier.

## Does Meridian Suite offer multi-year contracts?

Enterprise plans can be contracted for multi-year terms with locked-in
pricing -- this is arranged with your account manager, not through
self-service billing settings. Individual and Team plans are month-to-
month or annual only.

## What happens to my billing if I'm on a free trial and don't add a card?

Trials that never add a payment method simply expire at the end of the
14-day period with no charge -- you're moved to a restricted free state
rather than being auto-billed. Adding a card during or after the trial
starts a normal paid subscription from that point.
