# Shipping & Delivery (Hardware Orders)

Meridian Suite sells a small line of hardware (graphics tablets,
styluses, and a compact dock/charging stand) alongside the software
subscription. This article covers physical orders only -- software
licenses activate instantly and are not "shipped." For placing, changing,
or cancelling any order (including hardware), see Order Management; this
article focuses specifically on delivery mechanics and timelines.

## Delivery timelines

- **Standard shipping**: 5-7 business days, free on orders over $50
  (a flat $4.99 fee applies below that).
- **Express shipping**: 2-3 business days, $12.99 regardless of order
  size.
- **Overnight**: next business day if ordered before 2pm local time,
  $29.99. Orders placed after 2pm are queued for next-day processing,
  so overnight delivery lands two days out in that case.

These timelines apply to the contiguous US. International shipping is
available to Canada, the UK, and the EU with standard delivery only
(10-15 business days) -- express and overnight aren't offered
internationally due to customs processing variability.

## What we ship

- Graphics Tablet Pro ($249.99) and Graphics Tablet Mini ($149.99)
- Meridian Stylus (included with tablets; sold separately for $39.99)
- Meridian Dock, a charging/display stand ($59.99)

Software subscriptions never appear in this shipping flow -- they're
provisioned instantly to your account at checkout, with no separate
"delivery" step.

## Order statuses

- `processing` -- payment confirmed, preparing shipment. Typically 1
  business day before an order moves to `shipped`, longer during
  high-demand periods (e.g. the week around a product launch).
- `shipped` -- left the warehouse; a tracking number is emailed
  immediately and also visible on the order detail page.
- `delivered` -- the carrier has confirmed delivery. If a package shows
  `delivered` but you haven't received it, wait 24 hours (carriers
  sometimes scan slightly early), then file a ticket if it still hasn't
  arrived -- we'll investigate with the carrier and reship or refund.
- `cancelled` -- order cancelled before shipment, no charge or a full
  refund of any charge already made.
- `refunded` -- order refunded after a cancellation or a completed
  return.

## Where is my order?

Use the order-status lookup in the support chat (just ask "where is my
order") or check **Account Settings > Orders**. Tracking numbers are
emailed once an order moves to `shipped`, and the same tracking link is
shown on the order detail page so you don't need to dig through email.

## Can I cancel or change my order?

Orders can be cancelled or modified free of charge while still
`processing` -- see Order Management for the full change process. Once
an order is `shipped` it cannot be cancelled or redirected; use the
standard return process instead (30-day window from delivery, prepaid
return label included in the box and also available for reprint from
**Account Settings > Orders > [order] > Return**).

## Returns and exchanges

- **Return window**: 30 days from the delivery date.
- **Condition**: items should be in resellable condition with original
  packaging for a full refund; used items with normal wear may receive a
  partial refund at our discretion.
- **Exchanges**: to swap for a different model (e.g. Tablet Mini ->
  Tablet Pro), return the original and place a new order rather than
  requesting a direct exchange -- this is faster since it doesn't wait on
  the returned item arriving first.
- **Refund timing for returns**: once the returned item is scanned in at
  our warehouse, the refund is issued within 3-5 business days to the
  original payment method.
- **Defective items**: if hardware arrives damaged or stops working
  within the return window, file a ticket with photos/description -- we
  cover return shipping for defective items (unlike a standard
  change-of-mind return, which uses the prepaid label but is otherwise
  self-service).

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I change my shipping speed after ordering?**
Only while the order is `processing` -- go to **Account Settings >
Orders > [order] > Modify** and reselect the shipping method; you'll be
charged or refunded the price difference.

**Do you ship to PO boxes?**
Standard shipping only; express and overnight require a street address
since they use signature-required couriers.

**What if I'm not home for a signature-required delivery?**
The carrier leaves a redelivery notice and attempts again the next
business day, typically up to 3 attempts before the package is returned
to us (at which point we'll refund or reship based on your preference).
