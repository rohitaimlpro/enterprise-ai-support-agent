# Refund Policy

This article covers every case where Meridian Suite issues a refund: new
purchases, plan changes, renewals, add-ons, and hardware. For cancelling
an order itself (as opposed to getting money back), see Order Management.
For where the money actually goes and how long it takes, see the
"Refund timelines" and "Tracking a refund" sections below.

## Standard refund windows

- **Within 14 days of purchase**: Full refund, no questions asked, for
  any first-time subscription purchase (Individual, Team, or Enterprise).
  This applies whether you paid monthly or annually -- the 14-day clock
  starts at the original purchase date, not the billing cycle.
- **Annual plans billed monthly**: If you cancel before the end of your
  12-month commitment, you may request a prorated refund for the unused
  months, minus a $10 early-termination processing fee. Example: you're 4
  months into a 12-month term paying $27.99/month for Team -- cancelling
  refunds roughly 8 months of unused fees minus the $10 fee.
- **Annual plans billed annually**: The same 14-day full-refund window
  applies to the upfront annual charge. After 14 days, annual-billed-
  annually plans are non-refundable for the remainder of the term, but
  you can still let the plan lapse at renewal (see "Auto-renewal
  refunds" below) instead of continuing it.
- **Renewals (auto-renewal charges)**: Refundable for up to 7 days after
  the renewal date, provided the seat was not actively used during that
  period (defined as: no sign-ins, no file edits, no API calls from that
  seat). If the seat was used even once, the renewal charge is treated as
  consumed and is not eligible for this window -- you can still cancel to
  stop future renewals.
- **Add-ons and one-time purchases** (extra cloud storage, premium font
  packs, priority render queue): Non-refundable once activated, since
  these unlock immediately and typically get used within the billing
  period. If you added one by mistake and haven't used it at all, file a
  ticket within 24 hours of purchase and we'll review it case by case.
- **Hardware** (tablets, styluses): Not covered by this policy -- hardware
  uses the 30-day return process described in Shipping & Delivery, not a
  cash refund of the type described here.

## Downgrades and partial-term changes

Downgrading a plan (e.g. Team to Individual, or removing seats) does not
trigger an automatic refund for the current billing period -- see
Subscription & Billing FAQ for how downgrade timing works. If you believe
you're owed a refund because you downgraded shortly after a renewal,
that falls under the 7-day renewal window above, not a separate
downgrade policy.

## How to request a refund

1. Open a support ticket (or just ask the assistant "I want a refund for
   order X") with your order ID and the reason for the refund.
2. We review eligibility against the windows above -- most requests that
   clearly fall within a window are auto-approved; edge cases go to a
   billing specialist, typically within 1 business day.
3. Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method within
   5-10 business days. Bank transfer (Enterprise NET-30 invoicing)
   refunds can take longer -- typically 10-15 business days -- since they
   route through your finance team's AP process.
4. Enterprise (multi-seat, invoiced) accounts should contact their
   account manager directly rather than filing a self-service ticket,
   since Enterprise refunds often involve a contract amendment.

## Exceptions -- when a refund will be denied

- Accounts suspended or terminated for a Terms of Service violation
  (abuse, fraud, sharing a single seat across many people) are not
  eligible for any refund on the remaining term.
- Usage-based overage charges (for example, extra render-queue minutes
  billed beyond your plan's included quota) are final and non-refundable,
  since they reflect resources already consumed.
- Refund requests made after all applicable windows above have closed are
  denied by default, though Enterprise accounts with a signed support SLA
  may have different terms -- check your contract or ask your account
  manager.
- Chargebacks filed with your card issuer while a refund request is
  already in progress will pause the refund process until the dispute
  resolves, to avoid double-refunding.

## Tracking a refund

Once requested, a refund's status (`requested`, `processing`, `paid`) is
visible under **Account Settings > Billing > Refund History**, or ask the
assistant to check your order or ticket status. Each status maps to:

- `requested`: we've received it and it's in the eligibility-review queue.
- `processing`: approved, payment is being returned to your original method.
- `paid`: the refund has been sent; card refunds typically show on your
  statement within 3-5 additional business days after this point.

If it's been more than 10 business days since a refund moved to `paid`
and it still hasn't appeared on your statement, file a support ticket
referencing the original ticket number -- this is usually a card-issuer
delay rather than an error on our side, but we can provide a transaction
reference to help your bank locate it.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I get a refund and keep using the product until it expires?**
No -- requesting a refund immediately revokes access to the paid features
tied to that charge, since the refund reflects that period no longer
being paid for.

**I was charged twice by mistake -- is that a refund or a billing error?**
Duplicate charges in the same billing cycle are typically a billing error,
not a refund case -- see "Why was I charged twice this month?" in the
Subscription & Billing FAQ, which resolves faster than a refund request.

**Do refunds affect my ability to use a free trial again?**
Refunding a purchase does not restore trial eligibility -- free trials are
one-per-account regardless of refund history.
