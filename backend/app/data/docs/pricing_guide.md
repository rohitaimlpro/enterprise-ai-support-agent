# Pricing Guide

## Plan tiers

| Plan       | Seats     | Monthly price / seat | Annual price / seat    | Includes                                  |
|------------|-----------|-----------------------|--------------------------|--------------------------------------------|
| Individual | 1         | $19.99                | $16.66 (billed yearly)   | Core apps, 100GB cloud storage            |
| Team       | 2-50      | $27.99                | $23.33 (billed yearly)   | Core apps, 1TB storage, admin console     |
| Enterprise | 50+       | Custom                | Custom                   | SSO, dedicated support, SLA, audit logs   |

"Core apps" means the full Meridian Suite creative and productivity app
bundle -- there is no cut-down or trimmed version at the Individual tier;
every plan gets the same apps, the differences are seats, storage,
admin/security features, and support level.

## What each tier adds over the last

- **Individual -> Team**: multi-seat billing, a shared admin console for
  managing users, shared team libraries/asset folders, and 10x the cloud
  storage (100GB -> 1TB, shared across the team by default).
- **Team -> Enterprise**: SSO (SAML 2.0 / OIDC, see Account Security
  FAQ), a dedicated support contact and SLA-backed response times (see
  Contact & Escalation), audit logs for admin actions, and custom
  contract terms including invoiced billing.

## Add-ons

Available on any plan, billed alongside your subscription:

- **Extra cloud storage**: $9.99 / 1TB / month, stacks with your plan's
  base storage (e.g. Team + one add-on = 2TB total).
- **Premium font & template pack**: $4.99 / month, unlocks the full
  licensed font library and premium design templates.
- **Priority render queue**: $14.99 / month, moves your export/render
  jobs ahead of the standard queue during high-traffic periods.

Add-ons activate immediately and are billed on the same cycle as your
plan; see Refund Policy for why they're non-refundable once activated.

## Discounts

- **Annual billing**: roughly 16% cheaper than paying monthly (see the
  table above for exact per-seat annual pricing).
- **Education**: 60% off Individual and Team plans for verified students
  and teachers -- verify via **Account Settings > Plan & Billing > Apply
  Education Discount**, which checks your school email or a document
  upload.
- **Nonprofit**: 50% off Team plans for verified 501(c)(3) (or
  international equivalent) organizations -- apply via the same page,
  verification typically takes 1-2 business days.
- **Volume**: Enterprise pricing includes tiered discounts starting at
  50 seats, with steeper discounts at higher seat counts; contact sales
  for a quote above 200 seats. Multi-year Enterprise contracts (see
  Subscription & Billing FAQ) can lock in a rate for the contract term.

Discounts don't stack automatically -- Education and Nonprofit discounts
apply to the base plan price only, not to add-ons, and can't be combined
with each other on the same seat.

## Free trial

All new accounts get a 14-day free trial of the Team plan (so you can
evaluate multi-seat and admin features even if you'd normally buy
Individual). No credit card is required to start a trial; a card is
required to convert to a paid plan. If the trial ends without a card on
file, the account moves to a restricted free state rather than being
charged automatically -- see Subscription & Billing FAQ.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is pricing the same worldwide?**
Prices above are listed in USD for the US market. Pricing in other
currencies is shown automatically based on your billing address and may
differ slightly due to local taxes and currency conversion, not a
different underlying price list.

**Do prices include tax?**
No, sales tax / VAT is calculated at checkout based on your billing
address and shown as a separate line item on your invoice (see Payments
& Invoices).

**Can I get a custom quote below 50 seats?**
Custom (Enterprise-style) pricing is only available at 50+ seats. Below
that, Team plan self-service pricing applies, though volume-adjacent
requests (e.g. 40 seats with specific security needs) can sometimes be
routed to sales -- ask the assistant to escalate if you're in this
situation.

**Does the price ever change after I subscribe?**
We may adjust list prices over time, but existing subscribers are
notified at least 30 days before any price change takes effect on their
account, and annual-plan subscribers keep their locked-in rate until
their next renewal.
