# Contacting Support & Escalation

Covers how to reach us and what to expect once you do. For filing a
formal complaint about a bad experience specifically, see Feedback &
Reviews (this article covers the escalation mechanics; that one covers
where complaints are routed and why).

## Talking to this AI assistant

This chat is the fastest way to get answers to policy, billing, and
order questions 24/7 -- it searches our documentation and looks up your
orders and tickets directly, rather than reading from a fixed script. It
can also take action on your behalf: creating tickets, checking or
cancelling orders, filing refund requests, and scheduling callbacks,
without you needing to navigate the settings pages yourself.

## Reaching a human agent

Ask the assistant to "talk to a human" or "speak to a person" at any
time, or if it can't resolve your issue after searching the knowledge
base, it will automatically escalate for you rather than guessing or
leaving you stuck. Either way, this opens a high-priority support ticket
and a human agent follows up, typically within a few hours (faster for
security-tagged issues, see Account Security FAQ for the 1-hour SLA on
those).

You don't need to repeat your issue to the human agent -- the full chat
transcript and any ticket details are attached automatically, so they
start with full context.

## Phone support

Phone support is available for Team and Enterprise plans; ask the
assistant to "schedule a callback" and provide your preferred time and
phone number, or request one from **Account Settings > Support >
Schedule a Callback**. Callbacks are typically scheduled within the same
business day for Team plans, and same-hour for Enterprise accounts with
an SLA. Individual-plan users can reach us via chat or email; phone
support isn't available at that tier, since call volume is reserved for
paid multi-seat accounts to keep wait times low.

## Email support

If you'd rather not use chat, email `support@meridiansuite.example`
directly -- this creates a ticket the same as a chat escalation, just
without the assistant's initial triage, so response times are slightly
longer (see below). Replying to any existing ticket notification email
adds your reply to that same ticket thread rather than creating a new
one.

## Response times

- **Chat escalations**: human follow-up within 1 business day (security
  issues: within 1 hour, 24/7, regardless of when you file).
- **Email tickets**: within 2 business days, since these skip the chat
  assistant's automatic triage and categorization.
- **Phone callbacks (Team/Enterprise)**: same business day, typically
  within a few hours of the requested time slot.
- **Enterprise accounts with a support SLA**: per your specific contract
  terms, typically faster than the standard tiers above -- check your
  contract or ask your account manager for your exact SLA.

These are target response times for a *first* human reply, not full
resolution -- complex issues (e.g. a billing investigation spanning
multiple charges) may take longer to fully resolve after that first
response.

## Filing a complaint

If you're unhappy with a resolution, ask the assistant to escalate, or
file a ticket describing the issue -- complaints are routed to a support
lead rather than a first-line agent, and are tracked separately from
standard tickets so a pattern of complaints (e.g. about a specific
policy) reaches product/policy teams, not just individual agents. See
Feedback & Reviews for the difference between a complaint (about a
specific bad experience) and a review (general product feedback).

## Frequently asked questions

**Is the AI assistant able to fully resolve billing disputes on its
own?**
It can check order/ticket status, process straightforward refunds within
policy (see Refund Policy), and create tickets, but genuinely disputed
charges (e.g. "I don't recognize this charge at all") are escalated to a
human by design, since they require judgment beyond policy lookup.

**Can I request a specific agent for follow-up?**
Not by name for standard support, since tickets are routed by
availability and specialization (billing, technical, security) rather
than by named individual. Enterprise accounts with a dedicated account
manager are the exception -- their tickets route to that specific person
by default.

**What happens if I don't respond to a ticket for a while?**
Tickets stay open indefinitely waiting on your reply; there's no
auto-close for customer-side silence, though we may send a check-in
reminder after 7 days of inactivity on an open ticket.
